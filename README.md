# 📊 Power BI Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regions, shipping methods, and payment modes.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data to identify key business trends and performance indicators.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of:

* Overall Sales Performance
* Profitability
* Quantity Sold
* Delivery/Shipping Performance
* Category & Sub-Category Performance
* Regional Performance
* Customer Segment Analysis
* Payment Mode Analysis
* Monthly Sales & Profit Trends
* State-wise Sales Performance

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify the most profitable product categories and sub-categories.
* Analyze sales performance across different regions and states.
* Understand customer segment contribution.
* Compare different shipping methods.
* Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
* Identify areas that may require business attention.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculations and KPIs
* **Data Visualization**
* **Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset**

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following major KPIs:

| KPI                      | Description                     |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| 💰 Total Sales           | Overall revenue generated       |
| 📦 Total Quantity        | Total number of products sold   |
| 💵 Total Profit          | Overall profit generated        |
| 🚚 Average Delivery Days | Average time taken for delivery |

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Category

Analyzes sales performance across major product categories and helps identify the strongest-performing categories.

### 2. Sales by Sub-Category

Provides a detailed view of sales across individual product sub-categories.

### 3. Sales by Ship Mode

Compares sales performance across different shipping methods.

### 4. Monthly Sales – YoY Comparison

Analyzes monthly sales trends and helps identify changes in sales performance over time.

### 5. Monthly Profit – YoY Comparison

Tracks monthly profitability and allows comparison of profit performance across years.

### 6. State-wise Sales Analysis

Uses a geographic visualization to understand sales distribution across different states.

### 7. Customer Segment Analysis

Analyzes sales contribution from different customer segments.

### 8. Regional Analysis

Compares sales performance across different geographical regions.

### 9. Payment Mode Analysis

Shows how different payment methods contribute to overall sales.

### 10. Interactive Filtering

Users can interact with the dashboard using filters/slicers to analyze specific regions and other business dimensions.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard can be used to answer questions such as:

* Which category generates the highest sales?
* Which sub-categories contribute most to revenue?
* Which regions have the strongest sales performance?
* Which customer segment contributes the most?
* How do sales change month over month and year over year?
* Which states generate higher sales?
* Which shipping method is most commonly associated with sales?
* How does profit change throughout the year?
* Which payment modes are most frequently used?
